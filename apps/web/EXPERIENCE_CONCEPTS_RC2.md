# CognixHR — Experience Cloud Surface Concepts (RC2)

> High-fidelity desktop + mobile concepts for all seven surfaces, in the RC2 design
> language (`EXPERIENCE_DESIGN_LANGUAGE_RC2.md`). **Design only — not for implementation.**
> Architecture, data, Core, IA, navigation, Moments, interaction principles: frozen.
> Each concept names its **emotional center · composition archetype · dominant focal point
> · memorable moment**, then desktop + mobile, then *why it's no longer the RC1 surface*.

Legend: `▓` atmosphere/light field · `◍` face (photo-backed) · `✦` milestone · `✨` ambient
AI line · `┃` the spine/line · `�sf` serif hero line.

floats on the canvas and rises in, staggered. The dawn field re-casts its light by time of

## 2 · MY STORY — *Reflection · Journey · Memory*

- **Archetype:** **The River** — a single continuous vertical line you descend through time.
  **Focal:** the glowing **"now" marker** at the top of an endless descending spine.
  **Memorable moment:** a **milestone blooming** as a lit station on the line. **Feeling:**
  *opening a beautifully designed life journal — never an event log.*

**Desktop** — full-bleed, asymmetric. A luminous vertical line runs down the page; chapters
hang off it; the right side breathes.
```

        ◍  Maya approved your leave               Mon
        ┃
        �native summary: "12 payslips · 3 days off"  (folded, a quiet pebble)
        ┃
   ━━━ THIS YEAR ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━  (a chapter divider crosses the line)
        ┃

not a log.

**No longer an event log:** the row-list becomes a *river of time* with blooming milestones
and chapter typography. Memory, not history.

## 3 · MY GROWTH — *Progress · Identity · Potential*

- **Archetype:** **The Ascent** — a rising path of milestones that *dominates* the page; the
  profile is a quiet anchor, not the subject. **Focal:** the **journey path** climbing to
  "now." **Memorable moment:** the path **lighting up as it ascends**. **Feeling:** a
  personal journey, not a profile.

**Desktop** — asymmetric: a slim identity anchor on the left, the **ascending journey** owns
the right two-thirds and rises diagonally.
```

## 4 · MY ATTENTION — *Calm control, not anxiety*

- **Archetype:** **The Clearing** — centred, vast negative space, the antithesis of an inbox.
  **Focal:** the **one** thing that needs you — or the all-clear. **Memorable moment:** the
  queue **dissolving into a single calm line**, or the full-screen **"you're clear" breath.**
  **Feeling:** reassurance.

**Desktop** — narrow, centred column on near-empty air (clarity = the lightest atmosphere).
```
▓ CLARITY — almost pure air, the faintest navy at the crown ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓

                         WHAT NEEDS YOU                       (centred eyebrow)
              �sf Two things — about three minutes.       (Fraunces, centred)
                    ✨ nothing urgent; coffee can wait

        NEEDS YOU NOW
        ┌ ◍ Maya · 3 days' leave                       Review → ┐
        ┌ ◷ Missing check-in · yesterday                  Fix → ┐

        CAN WAIT
        · March payslip is ready                          View →

        WAITING ON OTHERS
        ↻ your WFH request — with Maya since Tue

                 ✓ clear these and you're done.
```
…and the **memorable** state — the reward, given a whole screen:
```
▓ CLARITY ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓

                              ◉  ✓        (one soft mark, slow breath motion)

                     �sf Nothing needs you.      (Fraunces, large, centred)
                       You're all clear — enjoy the day.

                     (2 requests quietly in progress, below)
```
Radical restraint: one centred column, **enormous air**, serif headline, ≤2 action cards
visible at once (the only cards). As you act, items dissolve and the screen *opens up*
toward the all-clear — which is a designed, breathing, full-screen reward. It feels like
calm control; the inbox is gone.

**Mobile** — same centred calm; the all-clear fills the phone.
```
▓ CLARITY ▓
 WHAT NEEDS YOU
 □sf Two things,
    ~3 min.
 ◍ Maya · leave  →
 ◷ Missing punch →
 · Payslip       →
 ✓ then you're done
      —— or ——
   ◉ ✓
 □sf All clear.
 Enjoy the day.
```

**No longer anxiety:** a curated *clearing*, not a notification feed; the empty state is the
hero. Calm control.

## 5 · MY TEAM — *My people, not records*

- **Archetype:** **The Portrait Wall** — faces first, data second. **Focal:** the **cluster
  of your people's faces.** **Memorable moment:** a **celebrating teammate at full warmth**
  (88px face, warm light). **Feeling:** responsible for human beings, not resources.

is small type, never a table.

## 7 · MY ASSISTANT — *Confidence · Precision · Calm intelligence*

- **Archetype:** **The Spotlight** — one calm command surface on a deep ground (the only
  surface that may go dark). **Focal:** the **ask field and its single confident answer.**
  **Memorable moment:** the answer **resolving with one precise action.** **Feeling:** calm
  intelligence — *never a chatbot.*

**Desktop** — deep, focused, centred; not a page of cards, a single intelligent surface.
```
▓ INTELLIGENCE — deep navy ground, one teal spark ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓

                    ✨  ▶sf How can I help, Suraj?     (Fraunces, calm)

         ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
         │  Ask anything…                                    ⌘K  │  (the field)
         └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
            Leave balance · My payslip · Who's off today        (suggested)

   — after an ask, the answer RESOLVES in place: one line + one action —
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
            ✨ You have 12 leave days left — 8 casual, 4 sick.
                 ┌ 🌴  Apply for leave            → ┐   (ONE action card)
            "Was this helpful?"        Open conversation →   (fallback, quiet)
```
A **spotlight on a deep ground** — the one surface that may use a dark field, which makes it
feel like a distinct, intelligent instrument. The front door is **an ask field over
suggestions**, never an open chat thread. An answer **resolves in place**: a serif/confident
one-liner + exactly **one action card** that deep-links to do the thing. Conversation is a
quiet "Open conversation →" — never the entry. It feels precise and calm, the opposite of a
chatbot.

**Mobile** — a focused sheet summoned by ✨/⌘K (never a 6th tab); the answer + action fills
the thumb zone.
```
▓ INTELLIGENCE (deep) ▓
   ✨ How can I help?
 ┌ Ask anything…   ┐
 Leave · Payslip · Off
   —— answer ——
 ✨ 12 leave days left
 ┌ 🌴 Apply leave  →┐
 Open conversation →
```

**No longer a chatbot:** a precise command instrument — ask → confident answer → one action.
Conversation is the fallback, intelligence is the feeling.

## Cohesion check — *one family, seven souls*

| | Day | Story | Growth | Attention | Team | Company | Assistant |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Archetype** | Front page | River | Ascent | Clearing | Portrait wall | Field | Spotlight |
| **Light** | Dawn (warm, timed) | Memory (deep) | Ascent (rising) | Clarity (air) | Warmth (amber) | Belonging (bright) | Intelligence (dark) |
| **Focal** | greeting + today | the now-marker | the rising path | the one thing / clear | the faces | celebrations | the answer |
| **Memorable** | shifting dawn | a milestone blooms | the path lights up | the all-clear breath | a warm 88px face | the living pulse | the precise resolve |
| **Density** | airy | linear | diagonal | vast air | warm cluster | **dense mosaic** | focused spotlight |

**Constant across all seven** (the family): the Fraunces serif emotional line · the navy+teal
base · atmosphere-light · borderless floating depth · faces-as-interface · the ✨ ambient
sentence · one type scale, grid, easing. **Different in all seven** (the souls): archetype,
light temperature, focal point, density, motion, memorable moment.

> Seven rooms, one home, different light in each. See any one of these screens and the first
> thought is no longer *"a nice HR app"* — it's *"I've never seen enterprise software feel
> like this."*
